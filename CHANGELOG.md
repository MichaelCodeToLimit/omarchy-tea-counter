# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Bar label showing today's caffeine load, cups, both, or icon only with Nerd Font tea icon (`󰶞`).
- One-tap preset logging (My usual / Green tea / Herbal nightcap), with editable presets and a custom type/size/style picker.
- Backfill any past day and time.
- Per-entry removal from today's list, plus undo.
- Mon–Sun week strip scaled to the week's own peak, with week, month, and year totals.
- Configurable day rollover hour (default 4am) and caffeine baseline (default 47mg per full cup of black tea).
- CSV export to `~/tea-history.csv`, and an `omarchy-tea` CLI that shares the same log.
- Fully theme-driven: no hard-coded colors, fonts, or spacing.
